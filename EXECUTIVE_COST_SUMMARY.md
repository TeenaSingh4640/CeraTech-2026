#### 🔴 PRIORITY 1: CONCRETE (₹8.5M savings)

**Strategy A: Fly Ash Replacement**
```
Current:  100% OPC cement
Optimized: 30% fly ash replacement
───────────────────────────────────
Savings:   15-20% on concrete cost
Amount:    ₹8.5 Million
Timeline:  Immediate
CO₂ Cut:   30% reduction
```
**Implementation:** Specify fly ash blended cement in tender

---

**Strategy B: Slab Thickness Reduction**
```
Current:  300mm solid slab
Optimized: 250mm with post-tensioning
───────────────────────────────────
Savings:   10% concrete volume
Amount:    ₹5.6 Million
Timeline:  Design stage
Benefit:   Lower dead load = smaller columns
```
**Implementation:** Use PT for spans > 7.5m

---

**Strategy C: High-Strength Concrete (Selective)**
```
Current:  M50 columns (all floors)
Optimized: M60 in floors 1-3 only
───────────────────────────────────
Savings:   Reduce column size by 100mm
Amount:    ₹3.2M + 1-2% area gained
Timeline:  Immediate
Benefit:   More usable space
```
**Implementation:** M60 lower floors, M50 upper

---

#### 🔴 PRIORITY 2: STEEL (₹6.7M savings)

**Strategy A: Higher Grade Steel**
```
Current:  Fe 500 (all members)
Optimized: Fe 550 (columns only)
───────────────────────────────────
Savings:   18% less steel by weight
Amount:    ₹6.7 Million (net)
Timeline:  Immediate
Benefit:   Smaller bar diameters
```
**Formula:**
```
Required Ast ∝ 1/fy
Fe 550 vs Fe 500: 500/550 = 0.91 (9% less area)
Weight saving: 12-15% after detailing
```

---

**Strategy B: Optimize Reinforcement**
```

Savings:   8-10% on steel

Timeline:  Bar bending schedule stage
Benefit:   Less congestion, better concrete
```
