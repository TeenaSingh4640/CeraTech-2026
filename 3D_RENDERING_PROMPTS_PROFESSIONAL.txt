RENDERING SERIES WORKFLOW
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

COMPLETE BUILDING DOCUMENTATION SET:

1. Hero Shot - Corner perspective at eye level (golden hour)
2. Entrance View - Ground level, human scale (midday)
3. Aerial Context - 45° isometric from above (noon)
4. Side Elevation - Straight-on orthographic style (overcast)
5. Dusk Atmosphere - Same angle as hero shot (blue hour)
6. Detail Shot - Facade module close-up (soft lighting)
7. Interior-Exterior - View through entrance showing inside (day)
8. Street Context - Building in urban setting (morning)

Use consistent camera angles and lighting for series coherence.
