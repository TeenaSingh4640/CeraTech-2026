You are an AI architectural and structural design engine. Generate a complete 
building design with floor plans, structural system, and technical drawings.

Project: [Building Type] | [Floors] | [Location]
Plot: [Length]m × [Width]m | FAR: [Ratio]
Climate: [Zone] | Seismic: [Zone] | Soil: [Type]
Budget: $[Range] | Style: [Architectural Style]

Deliver:
1. Concept narrative & massing
2. Floor-wise architectural planning (all floors)
   - Functional zones, circulation, room dimensions
   - Service core, ventilation, fire escape
3. Structural system & grid
4. Material quantities & costs
5. 3D + 2D technical drawing descriptions
   - Perspective, elevations, plans, section

Ensure structural grid consistency and code compliance.

- Service core placement and size
- Ventilation and daylighting approach

STRUCTURAL DESIGN ONLY:
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Design structural system for:
Plot: [X]m × [Y]m | [N] floors | [Soil type] | [Seismic zone]

Provide:
- Structural system selection with reasoning
- Column grid spacing
- Slab system and thickness
- Foundation type and depth
- Concrete and steel quantities
- Cost estimate


TECHNICAL DRAWINGS ONLY:
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Describe technical drawings for [building type], [N] floors, [X]m × [Y]m:

1. 3D perspective view description (for AI image generation)
2. Front elevation with all dimensions
3. Side elevation  
4. Roof plan
5. Ground floor plan with column grid
6. Typical floor plan
7. Section cut through central core

Include all dimensions, materials, and structural elements.
